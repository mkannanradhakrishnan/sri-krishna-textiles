OUR STORY

Sri Krishna Textiles is located in Punjai Puliyampatti. It was started by PS Krishnaswamy in the year 1992 with an amount of only Rs. 1000 in hand. He purchased high-quality and good-quality clothes from Erode. He is the most honest and respectable person in the town. Within a few years, the shop became successful and reached people in the region of about more than 100 square kilometres. The quality of the clothes and the honesty of Krishnaswamy made the shop grow well, and the shop has now stepped into its 34th year.
